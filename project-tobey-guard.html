Tobey Guard

A fitted 3D-printed guard designed to keep my cat from pressing the controls on a water cooler.

The problem

My cat, Tobey, could reach the controls on a water cooler and press the buttons. I wanted a small physical guard that blocked accidental button presses without changing the water cooler itself.

Fit-driven design

The project was driven by the existing shape of the control area, so the guard had to fit around that geometry instead of being designed in isolation. I worked through the cover shape, connector features, and fit adjustments needed for a practical printed part.

I also used slicer-based connector features and adjusted the fit between mating sections so the guard could be printed and assembled without being excessively loose.

What it taught me

This was a small project with a very specific user requirement: prevent access to one set of controls while leaving the appliance usable. It was good practice in designing around an existing product, accounting for fit, and turning an everyday problem into a simple mechanical solution.
